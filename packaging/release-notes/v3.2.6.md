## What's new in 3.2.6: crop and resize, and two more capture cards

**Crop and resize the console picture.** Cut any side of the picture and choose how large and where it is
shown, the way a streaming program's crop handles work. In the quick setup, **Crop and resize → Adjust…**
opens an editor on one frame of your game.

- **Drag an edge** to cut that side, **a corner** to cut two, **the middle** to slide the part that is kept.
  Double-click to get the whole picture back.
- **Numbers** for the four sides, in percent (at most 45 % per side, so part of the picture always stays).
- **Shapes**: 16:9, 21:9, 32:9, 4:3 and 1:1, cut evenly around the middle. A 21:9 game with black bars inside
  the console's 16:9 signal becomes a real 21:9 picture and fills a 21:9 screen.
- **Size on screen** from 25 % to 400 %, and **Position**.
- The picture on screen follows every change at once. *Wide-screen fill* (bars, crop, stretch) then works on
  the part that is kept.
- The switch in the quick setup shows the whole picture again and keeps your values. Console and game
  profiles remember the crop.
- Recordings, instant replay and streams keep the whole picture, unless you switch on **Crop recordings and
  streams too** in the editor.

Only the drawing is cut. Frame generation, AI picture enhancement and HUD protection still work on the whole
picture, so nothing about them changes, and with no crop set the picture is drawn exactly as before. The crop
is for a console picture (a capture card, or PS5, Sunshine and Xbox streaming). It does not apply with XeSS
frame generation, which presents through its own path.

**Two capture cards reported working.** Thanks to two users' capture card reports, the **Elgato HD60 S** and
the **AVerMedia Live Gamer Ultra 2.1 (GC553G2)** are now listed as *Reported working by a PushFPS user*, in
the quick setup's capture card row and in the README. A report is shown as a report, not as our own test.
Have another card? The **Report** button next to your card's name opens the form.

**A capture card window that keeps waiting now says so.** One user reported the capture card window staying
busy for ever, with nothing in the log. We could not reproduce it and have not found the cause yet. This
version logs each capture device before and after its driver is asked, so the next log names the device that
does not answer, and after ten seconds the window says the devices have not answered and what to try
(close other programs that use the card or a camera, replug the device, restart PushFPS). This is a way to
find the fault, not a fix for it.

**How this was tested.** Every build is compiled and started on a Windows build machine without a graphics
card. In CI: the view's crop maths in every mode, zoom, rotation and picture shape, compared bit for bit with
the 3.2.5 code when no crop is set; the editor (dragging an edge, a corner and the middle, numbers, shapes,
size, position, reset), the quick setup row and the profiles in the QML tests; the capture card list; the
install test; and screenshots of the real program playing a test pattern cropped and whole on Microsoft's
software renderer.
**Not tested on real hardware**: the crop has not been tried with a capture card, a console or a real
graphics card, with HDR, or with every frame generation method. If the picture looks wrong with a crop set,
switch *Crop and resize* off in the quick setup and please tell us. The capture device logging has not been
tried on the PC that showed the fault.

**Windows Defender note.** Defender may flag new PushFPS downloads as `Trojan:Win32/Tecabans.STV!cl`. This is a false positive from its cloud check on new, unsigned files, and it has been reported to Microsoft. Always check the zip against `SHA256SUMS.txt` on this page.
